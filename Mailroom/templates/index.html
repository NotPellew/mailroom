Welcome to Mailroom

Set up your local, private email organizer in 3 easy steps.

1. Sorting Goal
2. Hardware Profile
3. Ready

Step 1: Choose Your Mail Sorting Goal

Choose how Mailroom will organize and suggest labels for your incoming emails.

Comprehensive Inbox Organization Recommended

Suggests multi-axis labels across Kind (Newsletters, Personal, Updates, Receipts), Action Needs (Reply, Action), and Retention periods. Best for general email triage.

Invoice & Receipt Catcher Single-Target

Specifically targets and detects invoices, receipts, and bills (Rechnungen/Belege). Ideal for accounting, tax prep, and running fast on lightweight hardware.

Step 2: Choose Your Hardware & Speed Profile

Mailroom runs AI 100% locally on your computer. Pick the model that fits your machine.

Standard Profile (qwen2.5:7b) Default

Balanced speed and high accuracy (~4.7 GB model). Recommended for Apple Silicon Macs (M1/M2/M3/M4) or computers with 8–16 GB RAM / dedicated GPU.

Lightweight Profile (qwen2.5:3b) Fast

Fast classification optimized for laptops, older hardware, or computers with 4–8 GB RAM (~2.0 GB model).

Step 3: Ready to Go!

Review your setup choices. You can change these anytime in settings.
